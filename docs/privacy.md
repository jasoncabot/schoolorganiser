# Privacy notice

_Last updated: 23 September 2026_

School Organiser reads the school emails you forward to it and sends you a short weekly summary. This notice explains what we hold, why, and for how long.

## You opt in

We only process your data because you asked us to. You opt in in two steps:

1. You forward a school email to `hello@school.jasoncabot.com`.
2. You click the verification link we send back.

Until you've verified, nothing you forward is read or summarised. It is held for 7 days and then deleted. If you don't verify, we don't email you again unless you forward another email, and even then at most once a day.

## What we use your data for

We use it only to provide the service you signed up for:

- reading the emails and attachments you forward
- picking out dates, costs, locations and other things you need to know
- sending you and your household a weekly digest
- letting you sign in to the web page to see and manage your information

**We never sell your data. We never use it for marketing, advertising or profiling. We never share it with anyone except as described below.**

## What we hold

- **Your email address**, and the addresses of anyone you add to your household.
- **The emails and attachments you forward.** This can include the names of your children, their school and class, and anything else the school has written.
- **What we extract from those emails**, such as event dates, costs and locations.
- **The children you add** on the web page: a name (a nickname is fine), their school, year group and class. We use these only to work out which parts of a letter apply to your children.
- **Service records** such as when an email arrived and whether it was processed. These are used to keep the service running and to fix problems.

## How long we keep it

| Data                                                                      | Kept for                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Emails forwarded before you verify                                        | 7 days, then deleted                                             |
| Original emails and attachments, and the text we read from them           | 90 days after we receive them, then deleted automatically        |
| A temporary copy of a PDF attachment, while we turn its pages into images | Deleted as soon as that's done (at most 1 day)                   |
| Extracted information (dates, costs, locations)                           | 90 days after the date it relates to, then deleted automatically |
| Children and schools you add                                              | Until you delete them or close your account                      |
| Your email address                                                        | Until you remove it or close your account                        |
| Service logs                                                              | 7 days                                                           |

Pausing the digest does not delete anything. To delete everything, use **Delete my data** on the web page. It takes effect straight away.

## Who processes it

The service runs entirely on **Cloudflare** (Workers, Durable Objects, R2 storage, Workers AI and Email Service). Cloudflare processes the data on our behalf under its data processing agreement. That agreement includes safeguards for any processing outside the UK. The AI that reads your emails runs on Cloudflare's own infrastructure. Your data is not used to train AI models.

No other companies are involved.

## Legal basis

We rely on your **consent** (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), which you give by forwarding an email and verifying your address. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your data.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Most of this you can do yourself on the web page. For anything else, email **privacy@school.jasoncabot.com**.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## Changes

If this notice changes in a way that affects you, we'll tell you by email before the change takes effect.
